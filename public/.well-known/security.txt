# Security Policy

If you discover a security vulnerability within this project, please send an email to ethicalhck@heraldcollege.edu.np. All security vulnerabilities will be promptly addressed.

Please do not publicly disclose the issue until it has been addressed by our team.

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings.
